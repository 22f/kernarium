# Golden frames of the thermal wire (vsock 5559), the contract between the
# guest's thermal HAL (android/lineage-24/overlay/thermal) and the host
# (Andarium/AndariumCore ThermalProtocol.swift).
#
# One frame per line: `name: <hex bytes>`. A line that starts with `#` is a
# comment, and the comment above a frame says what it means.
#
# A record is 8 bytes, host to guest only: the magic `ATH1`, one byte with
# Android's severity (0 NONE, 1 LIGHT, 2 MODERATE, 3 SEVERE, 4 CRITICAL) and
# three reserved bytes, all 0.
#
# The host's encoder produces every frame that is not a `reject-` frame from
# the severity named. The HAL's decoder accepts those frames and refuses every
# `reject-` frame: it closes the connection and keeps the severity it had.

# NONE: the device is not hot.
none: 41 54 48 31 00 00 00 00

# LIGHT: iOS fair.
light: 41 54 48 31 01 00 00 00

# SEVERE: iOS serious; Android lowers the frame rate to 30.
severe: 41 54 48 31 03 00 00 00

# CRITICAL: iOS critical.
critical: 41 54 48 31 04 00 00 00

# --- Frames the HAL refuses

# EMERGENCY (5) is never reported.
reject-emergency: 41 54 48 31 05 00 00 00

# SHUTDOWN (6) is never reported.
reject-shutdown: 41 54 48 31 06 00 00 00

# The magic is ATH2.
reject-magic: 41 54 48 32 00 00 00 00

# A reserved byte is 1.
reject-reserved: 41 54 48 31 01 00 01 00
